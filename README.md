Helios UK — Homeowner Energy Intelligence Platform
Helios UK is an AI‑powered platform that helps UK homeowners understand, model, and optimise their home energy systems — including solar, battery storage, EV charging, tariffs, and whole‑home efficiency.
The goal: clarity, trust, and personalised insights without sales pressure.
